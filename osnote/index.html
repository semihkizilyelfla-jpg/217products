Over the screen, or on the page.

Screen Pen

A floating toolbar draws over whatever is open: pen, eraser, colours, undo and redo, and a hand mode that lets your touches pass through to the app underneath. The advanced toolbar adds shapes, a paper background and capture, which saves your writing, alone or with the screen behind it, to the app’s library.

Quick Notes

Notebooks with blank, lined, grid or dotted pages, A4 or screen-sized, upright or sideways. Pen, highlighter, eraser, lasso, ten shapes and pictures. Folders and favourites keep them in order; a notebook exports as a PDF, a page as a PNG.

With a stylus, palm rejection lets your hand rest on the glass while only the pen writes. In notebooks, the pen’s eraser end or button erases.

Questions

Where are my notes stored?

On your phone. They are not sent to our servers and we cannot see them. If Android backup is on, they can be included in your Google account’s encrypted device backup, which belongs to you.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Google is optional; you only need it to buy or restore Pro.

How do I get Pro back on a new phone?

Sign in with the Google account you bought it with, then tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?

In Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting your account in the app does not cancel it.

Why does it ask to draw over other apps?

That permission is what lets the pen draw on top of whatever is open. OSNote does not read or record your screen; a capture happens only when you ask for one, and it is saved on your phone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, under Settings → Account → Delete account. Your sign-in record is deleted and your notes stay on your device. Every step, and the e-mail route, is on the account deletion page.
