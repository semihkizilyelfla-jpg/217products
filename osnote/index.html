How it works

Pen and Hand

With Pen selected, ink appears wherever you touch. With Hand selected, your touches go to the app underneath: you play the video, you scroll the page. Either way, what you wrote stays on the screen.

  1. Tap Pen and draw where the pass will go.
  2. Tap Hand.
  3. Play the video: the game runs underneath, your drawing stays on top.

The pen always opens in Hand, so your first touch goes to the app.

Tools

What’s on the toolbar?

You drag the toolbar wherever you like, and it remembers one spot for upright and another for sideways. Pick a button from the list to see where it sits and what its panel looks like.

OFF

There are two toolbars. The standard one has Hand, Pen, Eraser, six colours, OFF and (undo, redo, hide, rotate on tablets, clear). The advanced one adds shapes, page, minimize and capture. The advanced toolbar is in the free version too; there it may show an ad when it opens, and with Pro it doesn’t.

    Questions

    Which apps can I write on?
    Almost any open app: video, PDF, the browser, maps, messages, the home screen. The pen sits on a layer of its own and never writes into the app itself. A few Android system screens, such as permission dialogs, are the exception: Android doesn’t show other apps on top of them.
    Why does it ask to display over other apps?
    So the pen can stay on top of whatever is open. OSNote doesn’t read your screen. A capture is taken only when you make one.
    Does my writing go away when I switch apps?
    No. The writing is on the screen, not in the app. It stays until you erase it or turn the pen off. Turning the pen off clears the ink, so it asks first.
    Do I need an account?
    No. Signing in with Google is optional, and only for buying or restoring Pro.
    Do my notebooks move to a new phone?
    There’s no cloud sync; notebooks stay on your phone. Android’s device transfer and encrypted backup (on devices that support them) carry them over too.
    Does the line get thicker when I press harder?
    No, pressure isn’t detected. The pen writes at one thickness, and you choose it.
    How do I get Pro back on a new phone?
    Sign in with the Google account you bought it with, then tap Restore purchases.
    Is it on iPhone?
    Not for now: it’s Android only, on Google Play.
