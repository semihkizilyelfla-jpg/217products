A coin for two. A die for a number. A wheel for many.

Dice

Pick d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 or d100, roll several at once, and add a bonus to the total.

Coin

Heads or tails, with a running count of how many of each have come up.

Wheel

Put your own options on it and spin, or start from a ready-made wheel. Keep the ones you use. After a spin, take the result, or strike it off and spin again.

Every roll, flip and spin goes into a decision log you can look back on, or switch off whenever you like.

Free, and nothing else.

No ads

Decidora is free and shows no ads. There is nothing to buy inside it.

No account

It never asks who you are. Open it and roll.

No internet

It has no network permission at all, so your decisions, your wheels and your settings stay on your phone.

Questions

Are the results really random?

Yes. Every result comes from your phone’s operating-system source of secure randomness, and no roll depends on the one before it.

Which dice can I roll?

d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100. You can roll several at once and add a bonus to the total.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Decidora has no internet permission and works fully offline.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your phone only, in the app’s own storage, and it is left out of Android backups. You can clear the decision log, your saved wheels and the coin count in Settings → My data, and uninstalling removes everything.

Can I turn the decision log off?

Yes, whenever you like, in the app’s settings.
