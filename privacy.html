Privacy

This page covers the website at 217products.com. The OSNote Android app has its own policy, linked at the foot of this page.

What this site stores

One value, in your browser’s local storage, under the key lang217. It holds either en or tr, and it is written only when you use the language switch in the menu. Its entire purpose is to stop sending you to the wrong language on your next visit. It is not an identifier, it is not read by anyone else, and it never leaves your device.

You can clear it at any time through your browser’s site-data settings. Nothing breaks if you do — the site simply asks your browser’s language again.

Audience measurement

The two main pages load Cloudflare Web Analytics, so we can see how many people visit and which pages they reach. It is deliberately the least invasive option we could find:

  • It sets no cookie and stores nothing on your device.
  • It does not build a profile, does not follow you between sites, and does not identify you. Cloudflare states plainly that it does not track individual visitors.
  • What it records is the page address, the referring page, your browser and operating system, your screen size, your country, and how quickly the page loaded. Your IP address is used to derive the country and is not stored by us.
  • There is no advertising, no remarketing and no data sale — Cloudflare processes this on our behalf and for nothing else.

This page and its Turkish counterpart carry no script at all — their content security policy is script-src 'none' — so reading the policy is not measured.

What this site still does not do

  • No cookies, of any kind.
  • No tag manager, no advertising pixels, no session or screen recording.
  • No advertising and no profiling.
  • No accounts, no forms, and no data submitted to us through this site.
  • No third-party requests beyond the two Cloudflare hosts named above. Every font, script, stylesheet and image is served from 217products.com itself, and the site’s content security policy blocks connections anywhere else.

Server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes the technical information a browser sends in order to serve a page — the request, and your IP address — and it does so as our hosting provider. We do not receive those logs, and we keep no server-side records of visits.

Links that leave this site

Two links here point elsewhere: the Google Play listing for OSNote, and the app’s own privacy policy. Once you follow one, that destination’s own privacy terms apply. We do not share anything with them; the only thing they can see is the ordinary referrer, which this site limits to the bare domain name.
